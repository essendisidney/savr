## Mission

Help every person make the smartest financial decision **before** they spend money.

## 10-year ambition

Build the infrastructure that powers **how Africa spends money** — expanding one category and one country at a time, destination intact.

## Positioning

Savr is not another cashback app, supermarket, or taxi aggregator.

It is the **trusted decision layer above spend** — the place consumers check first because it consistently helps them make better financial decisions.

Today people ask: Where is it cheaper? Is there a discount? Which taxi? Which supermarket?

Tomorrow they ask one thing: **Check Savr.**

## What the company actually is

| User sees | Company builds |
|-----------|----------------|
| One Spending OS (Compare · Save · Earn · Pay · Invest) | Consumer, Merchant, Business, API, AI, Wallet, Rewards, Intelligence, Ads, Finance, Protect, Community |

Consumers and businesses share the same decision graph. Developers embed it via API. Merchants compete by offering better **value**, not only louder ads.

## The problem

Consumers lose money daily because they don’t know:

- where prices are lowest
- what discounts exist
- which merchant gives rewards
- whether delivery changes total cost
- whether another payment method unlocks a better deal

Businesses benefit from that information gap. Savr closes it.

## Value proposition

Before spending, Savr answers:

- Where should I buy?
- Who is cheapest?
- Who is closest?
- How much will I save?
- Will I earn cashback?
- Can I pay using rewards?

## Product thesis

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| Compare | Price, basket, ride, fuel — total cost of the decision |
| Rewards | **Savings Cashback** — reward the smarter choice, not any purchase |
| Wallet | Cashback, coupons, merchant credits, gift cards |
| AI | Predictive alerts and personal financial assistance |
| Merchant portal | Merchants compete on value, not only ads |
| Data network | Catalogs, receipts, partnerships — the real moat |

## Moat

The hard problem is not the app — it is the **data network**:

- Merchant integrations and verified catalogs
- User-submitted receipts and prices
- Loyalty and payment partnerships
- AI models on spending behavior (privacy-respecting)

More usage → better recommendations → more users and merchants. That is the flywheel.

## Phased vision

| Phase | Focus |
|-------|--------|
| **1 — MVP** | One city. Groceries (wedge) + fuel + rides. Compare, lists, cashback, wallet, merchant portal. Goal: 10,000 users. |
| **2** | Pharmacies, restaurants, electronics, fashion. Price history, barcode, wishlist, receipt scan. |
| **3** | Travel, hotels, insurance, utilities, banks. AI assistant. Subscriptions. |
| **4** | Everyday OS: voice, predictive shopping, financial planning, cross-border. |

## Long-term north star

Ask: “What’s the cheapest way to furnish a two-bedroom apartment in Nairobi?”

Savr builds the plan — furniture, delivery, assembly, financing, cashback, warranty — then states total cost and savings vs market average.

That is when Savr becomes an **AI-powered decision engine for spending**, not a shopping search tool.

Same pattern for kitchen renovations, school runs, SME procurement — any multi-step spend.
