# Savr Constitution

**The foundation for every employee, designer, engineer, partner, and investor.**

If vision is destination and architecture is the city plan, this is the law of the land — so the company can grow for decades without losing its core purpose.

## Mission

Help every person make the **smartest financial decision before they spend money**.

Brand line: **Before you spend, Savr it.**

---

## What we are building

Savr is the **neutral intelligence layer** for spending — the trusted place people check first.

We are **not** building a supermarket, taxi company, bank, payment provider, or delivery company. We help users decide across all of them, and we let those businesses plug into Savr.

The app is the entry. The company is the **decision graph**: prices, offers, rewards, intent, and recommendations — handled with consent and trust.

---

## Principles

1. **Decisions over transactions.** People want problems solved (feed the family, get to work, furnish a house). Purchases are how those decisions execute.
2. **Money Saved is the north star.** Obsess over KES saved for users. Downloads and revenue follow.
3. **Explainability.** Every recommendation must be understandable in money, time, or quality — never a black box that asks for blind trust.
4. **Neutrality.** We do not compete with the merchants and partners we need. We make their value visible.
5. **Trust beats monetization.** Sponsored or boosted offers may never win if net value to the user is worse — and boosts must be labeled.
6. **One category, one geography at a time.** Expand without shrinking the destination.
7. **Entities over screens.** Durable product is Consumer, Merchant, Product, Price, Promotion, Reward, Receipt, Location, Order, Review, Service — not page count.
8. **Consent is product.** Personalization and data use are privileges users can see and revoke.
9. **Ship the spine.** Density, honesty, and merchant reach before AI theatre, autopilot, or empire features.
10. **Long-term company.** Optimize for a Spending OS that can last decades, not a growth hack quarter.

## Product philosophy

| We prefer | We avoid |
|-----------|----------|
| Basket and total cost | Sticker price alone as “truth” |
| Top 3 with a clear why | Dumping 500 unsorted options |
| Net value ranking | Opaque “relevance” ads |
| Merchant-uploaded + verified prices | Pretending scrape = ground truth |
| Valuable notifications | Spam that trains users to mute us |
| Confidence on predictions | Guesses presented as facts |
| Partner rails for pay/deliver | Becoming the rail ourselves early |

**Three questions before any feature ships**

1. Does it help the user spend less or get better value?  
2. Does it help merchants reach customers fairly?  
3. Does it strengthen the data and recommendation engine?  

If none, it does not belong.

---

## User trust commitments

- Show savings the user can verify (merchant, price, distance, coverage).
- Never hide that an offer is paid / sponsored.
- Prefer “we don’t know yet” over a fake precise price.
- Prefer fewer, better alerts over engagement farming.
- Keep wallet and cashback ledgers auditable (append-only credits via trusted paths).
- Make it easy to export or delete personal data as the product and law require.
- M-Pesa and real money movement stay off until explicitly enabled and safe.

---

## Data ethics

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---------|-------------|
| Use consented profile + history to recommend better value | Sell raw household baskets or identity-level spend to third parties |
| Aggregate, anonymized marketplace intelligence for partners | Surreptitious twin-building with no settings |
| Merchant sees own performance and fair demand signals | Letting paid placement beat worse net value |
| Event streams for product improvement under RLS/privacy | Training public models on private receipts without policy |

**Intelligence Layer rule:** external insights are aggregated. Individual digital twins stay with the user.

---

## Design language (product)

- Lead with **brand and clarity**, not dashboard clutter.
- One job per screen: compare, earn, or act — not all at once.
- Savings and recommendations in plain Kenyan English / clear numbers (KES).
- Atmosphere and craft matter; gimmicks and dark patterns do not.
- Mobile-first for Nairobi households; merchant tools stay fast and boring-reliable.
- Accessibility and low-bandwidth respect are part of trust.

## Decision-making framework

| Decision type | Who | Rule |
|---------------|-----|------|
| Wedge / category focus | Founders | Groceries basket in Nairobi until habit proves out ([WEDGE.md](./WEDGE.md)) |
| New platform (Bank, Protect, AI Brain) | Founders + architecture gates | Open only when [ARCHITECTURE.md](./ARCHITECTURE.md) prerequisites are met |
| Monetization that touches rank | Product + trust review | Net value to user must still win; label boosts |
| Live payments / M-Pesa | Explicit founder go | Default dry-run |
| Geo expansion | After playbook repeats | One market at a time |
| “Cool” features vs spine | Engineering default | Spine wins (density, freshness, honesty loop) |

When unsure: **choose the option that increases Money Saved and Trust**, even if it grows slower.

## How to use this document

- **Hire / onboard:** read Constitution → Vision → Ecosystem → Architecture → Wedge.  
- **Design / eng:** three questions + trust commitments on every PR that touches rank, wallet, or data.  
- **Partners:** neutrality + labeled value; they win by being better for the user.  
- **Conflict:** Constitution overrides a flashy roadmap slide.

Amendments should be rare, written, and explicit — culture drifts by silence, not by debate.
